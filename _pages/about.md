Marie received a Computer Science engineering degree from the Technology University of Belfort-Montbéliard (France) in 2012.
During her studies, she did a semester abroad in Trois-rivières (Québec, Canada) and falling in love with the country she stayed for a research internship in Computer Vision followed by a master degree in Natural Language Processing. In 2017, she started a PhD on automated algorithm configuration at Leiden University (The Netherlands) under the supervision of Holger Hoos. She is now a postdoc at RWTH Aachen University (Germany) working on meta-learning, sustainable benchmarking and preferential sampling. Besides research, she cares about improving the inner dynamics of her community - for example by taking part to the Diversity Committee, co-founding the CAIRNE Rising Researcher Network and organising workshops on mental well-being for early career researchers. She also partakes in outreach initiatives toward future researchers such as pre-university or high-school students.
