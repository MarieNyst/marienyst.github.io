## Research Interests
======

My research focuses on **meta-learning** and **automated machine learning (AutoML)**, with particular emphasis on:

- **Automated Algorithm Configuration & Selection**: Predicting which algorithm works best on which problem instance and finding optimal configurations
- **Sustainable Benchmarking**: Developing methods to compare algorithm performance in a more sustainable and informative way
- **AI Safety**: Exploring robustness, explainability, privacy, and bias in AI systems
- **Multi-Objective Optimization**: Balancing accuracy with other important factors like robustness, training time, and inference time

I am particularly interested in **what we should optimize** rather than just **how we should optimize**, embracing the complexity of safe and trustworthy artificial intelligence.
